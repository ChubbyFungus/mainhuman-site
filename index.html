The middleman between your AIs

You talk to one AI. It talks to the rest.

Hand a task to Ed and he routes it to whichever of your agents fits: Claude, Codex, GPT. He can hand one agent's result to another for review. He keeps talking with you while the work runs, tells you what happened when it's done, and the result also lands in an inbox window you can check any time.

The context layer

mAIn human is wired directly into your own memory, which lives on your computer. Before any agent gets a task, Ed decides what context goes with it and leaves the rest out. Your coding agent stops drowning in stale notes and irrelevant history, so every agent he runs is faster and more accurate.

Frequently asked questions

What can mAIn human do today?
Ed lives on your desktop as a 3D head. You talk to him by voice, brainstorm out loud, and give him work. He pulls the relevant notes from your own memory, routes the task and that context to your agents like Claude, Codex and GPT, and reports back in conversation and in an inbox window.
What runs on my computer?
The voice, the face, and the memory layer run locally on your computer. Speech recognition, turning what you say into text, currently uses a cloud service. Routed tasks go to your own Claude, Codex or OpenAI accounts.
