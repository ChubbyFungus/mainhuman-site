Talk to Ed

Ed is a digital person who lives on your desktop as a 3D head — not a chat window.

  • Talk to Ed by voice, right at your desktop.
  • He speaks back with lip movement, not a wall of text.
  • Interrupt him whenever you want — he'll stop and listen.
  • Brainstorm with him the way you would with a person sitting next to you.

Give Ed work

Some things need more than talking through.

When a task calls for it, Ed pulls the notes that actually matter from your own memory and leaves the rest out. He hands that focused task, plus the relevant context, to Claude — through a task inbox — and keeps talking with you while it runs. When it's done, he tells you what happened, and the result also lands in an inbox window you can check any time.

Your memory stays yours

The notes and context Ed draws on live on your own computer. When he's working on a task for you, he picks out what's relevant to that task and leaves the rest alone.

Frequently asked questions

What can Ed do today?
Ed lives on your desktop as a 3D head. You can talk to him by voice, brainstorm out loud, and give him work. When a task needs real focus, he pulls the relevant notes from your own memory, hands the task and that context to Claude, and reports back — in conversation and in an inbox window.
What runs on my computer?
Ed's voice, his face, and his memory layer run locally on your computer. Speech recognition — turning what you say into text — currently uses a cloud service. When Ed hands off a task, it goes to your own Claude account.
