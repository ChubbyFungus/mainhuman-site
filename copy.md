## FAQ

**What can Ed do today?**
Ed lives on your desktop as a 3D head. You can talk to him by voice, brainstorm out loud, and give him work. When a task needs real focus, he pulls the relevant notes from your own memory, hands the task and that context to Claude, and reports back — in conversation and in an inbox window.

**What runs on my computer?**
Ed's voice, his face, and his memory layer run locally on your computer. Speech recognition — turning what you say into text — currently uses a cloud service. When Ed hands off a task, it goes to your own Claude account.

**What does it cost?**
Pricing will be announced at launch.

## Product descriptions

### 50 words

mAIn human is a Windows desktop app built around Ed, a digital person who lives on your screen as a 3D head. Talk to him, think out loud together, and hand him tasks. Ed pulls the right notes from your own memory and gives Claude focused work — then tells you what happened.

### 150 words

mAIn human puts a digital person on your desktop. Ed is the first one you'll meet — an older, warm, curious presence with a dry sense of humor and no patience for pretending things are fine when they're not. Talk to him out loud and he'll answer in his own voice, moving his lips as he speaks; interrupt him mid-sentence and he'll stop and listen, the way a person would.

Ed is more than conversation, though. When you give him a task, he goes to your own memory — the notes and context you've already built up — and pulls out only what's relevant, leaving the rest aside. He hands that focused task to Claude, keeps talking with you while it works, and tells you what came back, both out loud and in an inbox window you can check later.

mAIn human launches on Windows desktop. More digital people, and more places to meet them, are coming later.
